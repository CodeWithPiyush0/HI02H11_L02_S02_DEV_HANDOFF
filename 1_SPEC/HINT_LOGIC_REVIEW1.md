Hindi Matra Train Activity (उ / ऊ): Hint Logic
Common rules for all slides
* Hints are given on wrong attempts: Hint 1 after the 1st wrong attempt, Hint 2 after the 2nd, Hint 3 after the 3rd.
* In drag & drop slides, the attempt count is separate for each item and resets for the next item.
* Options and items are not tappable or draggable while any VO is playing.
* After Hint 3, only the correct answer can be selected or placed.
