# Art rules for this bundle

Flat vector, magenta-keyed, autocropped. Two scars encoded here: (1) NEVER pink or near-white without a thick dark outline — a white subject leaves keep_largest nothing to keep and the PNG comes out blank (obj_sapna). (2) Any spiky / rayed / multi-part subject must be ONE SINGLE CONNECTED SILHOUETTE — separated parts are separate components and all but the largest are discarded (obj_din, took three attempts).
