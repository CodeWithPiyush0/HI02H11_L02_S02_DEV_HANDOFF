> •Sequence should be:

## Screen 2 · MATRA_BUILD  पल → पुल

*Deck slide 4 · screenshot `03_MATRA_BUILD.png`*

> Recommendation
> VO:
> “आइए, देखें कि छोटी उ की मात्रा लगने से शब्द की आवाज़ कैसे बदलती है।”
> पहले:
> “यह शब्द देखिए — पल।”
> फिर:
> “प के साथ छोटी उ की मात्रा लगाने पर ‘पु’ बनता है।”
> अंत में:
> “अब ‘ल’ जुड़ने पर ‘पुल’ बनता है।”
> On Screen:
> •Keep the screen focused on only one word transformation.
> •First show पल clearly on the left side.
> •In the centre, show the transformation:
> प + ु = पु
> •Highlight only the ु मात्रा in red when it is introduced.
> •After that, show the final word:
> पुल
> •In पुल, highlight the ु मात्रा again so the child clearly notices where the matra is placed.
> •Show a simple and clear bridge image below or beside पुल.
> •Keep the existing mascot and overall UI/UX style unchanged.
> •Keep the screen clean and minimal. Do not add unnecessary explanatory text.
> Sequence:
> पल appears first.
> VO reads: “यह शब्द देखिए — पल।”
> Highlight प.
> ु appears beside/below प.
> Show: प + ु = पु
> VO says: “प के साथ छोटी उ की मात्रा लगाने पर ‘पु’ बनता है।”
> Then ल joins.
> Final word पुल appears.
> Highlight ु inside पुल.
> Bridge image appears.
> Animation:
> •Use a simple step-by-step animation.
> •Do not show all stages together at the beginning.
> •First show पल.
> •Then isolate/highlight प.
> •The ु मात्रा should softly pop/slide into its correct position below प.
> •As soon as the matra joins, visually transform:
> प → पु
> •Then ल can slide in to form:
> पु → पुल
> •After the word is completed, the bridge image can appear with a soft fade/pop animation.
> •Keep the animation synced with the VO.
> Sound Differentiation:
> Make the pronunciation difference very clear:
> प
> पु
> पुल
> Give a short pause between each sound so the child can hear how the sound changes after adding ु.
> SFX:
> •Add a soft pop when ु appears.
> •Add a light chime when प changes to पु.
> •Add a small success sound when पुल is completed.
> •Keep SFX subtle so the pronunciation remains clear.
> Next Button:
> •Keep the Next button disabled while the complete transformation is playing.
> •Activate it only after प → पु → पुल has been fully demonstrated and the bridge image has appeared.
> Note:
> •Please avoid awkward phrasing such as:
> “प में उ की मात्रा लगी, बना पु।”
> •Use:
> “प के साथ छोटी उ की मात्रा लगाने पर ‘पु’ बनता है।”
> •The key visual learning should remain:
> पल → प + ु = पु → पुल

> “हर शब्द को उसकी सही मात्रा वाले डिब्बे में डालिए।”

> “हर शब्द को उसकी सही मात्रा वाले डिब्बे में डालिए।”

> Drag and Drop
> The child drags each word card into the correct coach.
> •सुई → उ (ु)
> •गुड़ → उ (ु)
> •आलू → ऊ (ू)
> •सूरज → ऊ (ू)
> •More than one word can be placed inside each coach.
> •While dragging, the selected card should slightly enlarge.
> •When the card reaches a coach, that coach gets a soft highlight.
> Correct Answer
> •The word card snaps inside the correct coach.
> •Small confetti / sparkle animation appears around that coach.
> •The correctly placed card becomes locked and cannot be dragged again.
