### The सुबह reversal, stated plainly

Round 2's brief said सुबह was *deliberately* not illustrated, because "a sunrise at 104px is
indistinguishable from `सूरज`, which is also in the word bank". **Round 3 needs it anyway**: the
SME's sentence screens put सुबह on an option card beside दूध and मुकुट, where the child chooses by
picture. The old objection still stands and is now a real risk, because सूरज is also in the lesson
— so `obj_subah` must be drawn to read as a **sunrise over a horizon**, not as a sun on its own.
The horizon band is what distinguishes it; do not drop it. Check the two side by side at 66px
(the option-card size) before accepting either.

## Full illustrated scenes — 4, and they must NOT be keyed

New asset class in round 3. These are the large pictures on the four sentence screens, drawn from
`2_MOCKUPS/slide15_sentence_complete_ALL_FOUR.png`.

⚠️ **Do not run these through `gen_objects.py`.** That pipeline exists to cut a subject OUT of its
background with a magenta chroma key; a scene has a background **on purpose**, and keying one
either eats it or eats the subject. Generate them flat and full-bleed, with no magenta anywhere.

They are rendered inside a 366×288 rounded frame with `object-fit: cover`, so the subject must sit
near the centre and nothing important may touch the edges.

## Until they exist

`pic()` in the builder checks the disk: a key whose PNG is missing is simply not written into the
card, and the engine renders the **emoji fallback** instead. So the game runs, nothing 404s, and
the review deck shows an emoji where a picture belongs rather than a broken image.

**Drop the PNGs into `3_CURRENT_BUILD/assets/Images/` and re-run the builder** — the keys light up
by themselves, with no edit to the card or the script.
